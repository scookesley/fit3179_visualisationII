Data Dictionary for AIMS Long-term Monitoring Program: Crown-of-thorns starfish (Acanthaster planci) and benthos Manta Tow Data (Great Barrier Reef)

ID		Row ID
SECTOR		CA – Cairns
		CB – Capricorn-Bunker
		CG – Cape Grenville
		CL – Cooktown-Lizard Island
		IN - Innisfail
		PC – Princess Charlotte Bay
		PO - Pomey
		SW – Swain
		TO - Townsville
SHELF		I – Inshore GBR
		M – Mid-shelf GBR
		O – Outer-shelf GBR
AIMS_REEF_NAME	Reef name in AIMS database
GROUP		Response variable - HC = hard coral
REPORT_YEAR	Year in which the report was issued, YYYY.
DATE		Date of survey
MEAN		Mean estimate of cover from Bayesian Hierarchical Models
LOWER		Lower 95% credible interval from Bayesian Hierarchical Models
UPPER		Upper  95% credible interval from Bayesian Hierarchical Models
MEDIAN		Median estimate of cover from Bayesian Hierarchical Models
FULLREEF_ID	Unique Identifier assigned to each reef.
REEF_NAME	The reef name as shown in the Great Barrier Reef Gazetteer.
REEF_LAT	Latitude of reef site in decimal degrees.
REEF_LONG	Longitude of reef site in decimal degrees.
