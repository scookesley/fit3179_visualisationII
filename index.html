2 Marine Life

Australia's waters are home to over 33,000 known marine species. Of these, over 1,500 make their home solely in the Great Barrier Reef. The following section contains detailed data on four common Australian marine species of interest: The Humpack Whale, Green Turtle, White Shark, Eastern Blue Groper, and the Giant Australian Cuttlefish.
